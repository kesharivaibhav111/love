Sakshi,

I know sometimes my words or actions may hurt you, even when I never mean to. And I know that simply saying “sorry” cannot magically make everything okay.

I'm really, really sorry, Sakshi. ❤️

And if I could choose again, I'd still choose you. Every single time.
